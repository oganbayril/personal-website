Germany Real Estate Price Predictor | Python · XGBoost · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · scikit-learn

Live demo: germany-real-estate.oganbayril.com · Code: github.com/oganbayril/germany-real-estate-api

  • Built an end-to-end price-estimation service for apartments for sale in five German cities (Berlin, Hamburg, Köln, Leipzig, München): a robots-aware, rate-limited scraper feeds PostgreSQL, a shared feature pipeline trains an XGBoost regressor, and a FastAPI service serves predictions behind a web demo.
  • Built on 3,000+ scraped listings; on a held-out test set, half of estimates land within ±16% of the asking price (about 20% mean error). A plain-language model card on the demo states these limits and what the model cannot see (condition, year built, balcony, photos).
  • Added weekly guarded retraining, nightly backups and CI (ruff, pytest, pip-audit, 80+ tests); deployed on my own VPS with automatic HTTPS.

Typical vs Average Income Map | Python · Pandas · Plotly · Eurostat · OECD

Live map: typical-vs-average-income.oganbayril.com · Code: github.com/oganbayril/typical-vs-average-income

  • Built an interactive map of how far the typical person's income sits from the average (median-to-mean household income ratio) across 45 European and OECD countries, 2002-2024: in Türkiye the typical person has about 70% of the average income, versus 83-93% in most of Europe.
  • Used Eurostat as the default source and OECD only to fill gaps (hatched on the map) instead of averaging them; measured the difference between the two sources on 433 country-years (95% within ±0.03) and put that uncertainty on the page.
  • Placed colour tiers at natural gaps in the data and added a continuous colour view so the story does not depend on where the lines fall; logged suspicious series (Slovakia, North Macedonia, Türkiye) instead of smoothing them; deployed on my own VPS with HTTPS.

EU Energy Transition Analysis | Python · SQL · Pandas · scikit-learn · Plotly · Power BI · Eurostat API

Code: github.com/oganbayril/eu-energy-transition-analysis

  • Showed that although the EU's renewable share nearly tripled (7.2% to 19.8%) over 19 years, energy import dependency fell by only 1.4 percentage points, because dependency also depends on nuclear policy, domestic production and total demand.
  • Built a 27-country panel from the Eurostat REST API and trained regularised Ridge/Lasso models with expanding-window cross-validation; electricity price was the strongest positive driver of renewable share, and GDP growth was negatively associated with it within countries.
  • Delivered six Jupyter notebooks, a five-page Power BI dashboard (DAX measures, synchronised slicers) and an interactive Streamlit app.

Turkey Asylum Seeker Analysis | Python · Pandas · Plotly · Streamlit · UNHCR/HDX data

Code: github.com/oganbayril/turkey-asylum-analysis

  • Found a structural shift: Turkey absorbed a peak of about 130k foreign asylum applications in 2015 (Syrian crisis), while a separate, sustained outflow of Turkish citizens seeking asylum abroad emerged after 2016.
  • Built the cleaning-to-visualisation pipeline and an interactive Streamlit dashboard with animated choropleth maps.
